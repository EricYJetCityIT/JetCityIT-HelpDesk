Using the help desk
How do I submit a request?

Go to the home page and fill out the form with your name, email, a short subject, and a description of the issue. You can attach up to 4 screenshots (PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WEBP, 10 MB each) if that helps explain the problem.

How long until someone responds?

A technician typically follows up within one business day. If something is urgent (a full outage, a security concern, "can't work at all"), say so directly in the subject or description -- those are flagged for a faster look.

How do I check the status of my ticket?

Every ticket confirmation email includes a link that takes you straight to your ticket. You can also go to Track a ticket and enter your email -- we'll send a fresh link to your inbox showing every ticket filed under that address.

I need to add more information to a ticket I already submitted. What do I do?

Open your ticket from the tracking link in your confirmation email (or request a new one from Track a ticket) and add a reply -- no need to submit a whole new request. Replying also reopens the ticket automatically if it had already been marked resolved.

My ticket was closed, but the issue came back. What now?

If it's the exact same issue and the ticket was closed recently, go ahead and reply on it to reopen it. If it's been a while, or it feels like a new/different problem, it's usually faster for us if you submit a new ticket instead -- that way it isn't buried at the bottom of an old thread.

Common issues
I forgot my password / I'm locked out of my account.

Try your organization's normal "Forgot password" link first. If that doesn't work, or you're locked out entirely, submit a ticket with your username and which system you're trying to access, and we'll get you back in.

My computer is running slow.

Before submitting a ticket, try a full restart (not just sign-out) -- it fixes a surprising number of slowdowns caused by updates or leftover background processes. If it's still slow afterward, let us know what you were doing when you noticed it, and roughly how long it's been happening.

I can't connect to Wi-Fi or VPN.

Double-check you're using the correct network name and that Wi-Fi/VPN is actually toggled on. If you're off-site, confirm your internet connection works for other things first. Still stuck? Submit a ticket and mention whether this is happening at the office, at home, or both.

A printer or shared device isn't working.

Note which printer/device (by name or location) and the exact error message, if any. A photo of the printer's display or an error screenshot attached to your ticket helps us diagnose it faster.

I think I clicked something suspicious (phishing email, strange popup, etc.).

Submit a ticket right away and mark it urgent in the subject line -- don't wait. Include what you clicked or entered, if you remember, and avoid restarting your computer until we've had a chance to take a look.
