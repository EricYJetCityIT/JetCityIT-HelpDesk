Get the Staff app

Put the staff console on your phone's home screen. It isn't in the App Store or Google Play: it's the Help Desk website added to your home screen, so there is nothing to download from a store and it updates itself. It takes about a minute.

You're already using the app on this device. There's nothing to install.

Reading this on a computer? To install the app, open this page on your phone: helpdesk.jetcityit.com/staff-app.html

Install from the sign-in page, not from this guide. The steps below happen on the staff sign-in page. If you add this guide to your home screen instead, you'll get this guide, not the app.

iPhone

  1. Open the staff sign-in page in Safari.
    Open the staff sign-inOutlook, Teams and other apps open links in their own viewer, and an app can't be installed from there. If that happens, tap the share or ⋯ button and choose Open in Safari.
  2. On that page, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).If you don't see it, tap the ⋯ button at the bottom right first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.If you see an "Open as Web App" switch, leave it on. Then tap Add.
  4. Open HD Staff from your home screen (not from Safari) and sign in with your @jetcityit.com Microsoft account.
  5. Tap Turn on alerts at the top of the screen and tap Allow. A test alert should arrive within a few seconds.

You need iOS 16.4 or later (Settings, General, About shows your version). On an iPhone, alerts only work in the app you opened from the home screen, not in a Safari tab.

Android

  1. Open the staff sign-in page in Chrome.
    Open the staff sign-in
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open HD Staff from your home screen and sign in with your @jetcityit.com Microsoft account.
  4. Tap Turn on alerts at the top of the screen and tap Allow. A test alert should arrive within a few seconds.

Once you're signed in, the Get the app button at the top of the console also offers a one-tap Install.

Computer

  1. The staff console works in any browser. Open it and sign in with your @jetcityit.com Microsoft account.
    Open the staff sign-in
  2. In Chrome or Edge you can also install it as an app: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install.
  3. Alerts work on a computer too: click Turn on alerts at the top of the console and choose Allow.

Good to know

  • You sign in again each time you fully close the app. That is on purpose: nothing about our customers is stored on your phone.
  • Alerts show the ticket's subject only, never the message. Tap one to open that ticket.
  • New-ticket and reply alerts go to the whole team, the same as the emails.
  • To turn alerts off, tap the bell at the top of the app, then Turn off alerts.
  • If the app says you don't have access, ask an administrator to add you on the console's Access tab.

This guide is for Jet City IT staff. Customers have their own app and guide.
